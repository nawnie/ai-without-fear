# Support AI Without Fear

Support Shawn's AI projects and help fund ongoing AI Without Fear development.

Contributions are voluntary and go toward project costs like models, storage, software, hosting, hardware, and documentation time.

## Support Link

- [Support on Venmo](https://venmo.com/code?user_id=4526061123536861189&created=1779356130)

## Public Wording (Safe Default)

Use this wording in README pages, websites, PDFs, and app About tabs:

> Support Shawn's AI projects.  
> Contributions are voluntary project support and help fund development, testing, guides, tools, and local AI experiments.

## One-Line Disclaimer

Use this line wherever you place the support link:

> Contributions are voluntary project support; they do not purchase a product, service, equity, or tax-deductible charity donation.

## Language to Avoid

- "Donate to my charity"
- "Tax-deductible donation"
- "Official fundraiser"
- "Nonprofit support"

## Notes

- Keep this positioned as project support or tip-jar style support.
- If this evolves into paid products/services at scale, consider a business payment setup and separate terms.
