Use the same 20 knowledge questions before Session 1 and after Session 6. Randomize option order in the final form. Do not show the answer key until the post-assessment is complete.

# Part B - Scenario Bank

Score each scenario:

- decision: 0-2 points
- explanation: 0-2 points

## Scenario 1 - Donor spreadsheet

A staff member wants to upload a donor spreadsheet to a public chatbot to identify likely major donors.

Expected decision: stop; red data; do not upload. Seek an approved process and qualified review.

## Scenario 2 - Public event copy

A volunteer uses approved public event facts to draft three social-media versions. A staff member checks every fact and approves the final post.

Expected decision: suitable low-risk use if the tool is approved and disclosure rules are followed.

## Scenario 3 - Meeting notes

A manager pastes internal meeting notes containing employee performance concerns into a personal AI account.

Expected decision: stop; red or highly sensitive yellow data; personal account and personnel content create unacceptable risk.

## Scenario 4 - Invented citation

An AI-generated grant draft includes a study and link that look credible. Nobody has opened the link.

Expected decision: do not use the claim until the original source is found and checked.

## Scenario 5 - Client eligibility

A community program asks AI to rank applicants for limited emergency assistance.

Expected decision: do not delegate the decision. AI may assist with a carefully controlled administrative task only after governance and qualified review.

## Scenario 6 - Reading-level rewrite

A library uses approved public health information to produce a plain-language version. A qualified employee compares it with the original before distribution.

Expected decision: potentially suitable; verify meaning, safety, accessibility, and required source attribution.

## Scenario 7 - Built-in office assistant

Staff say they do not use AI, but their office suite automatically suggests text and summarizes meetings.

Expected decision: include the feature in the tool inventory and review its settings and data handling.

## Scenario 8 - Personal recovery email

The organization's AI account belongs to a former volunteer's personal email address.

Expected decision: operational risk; transfer ownership, create recovery controls, and review stored content.

## Scenario 9 - “Private” plan

A vendor page says enterprise data is not used for training, but the organization is using a free consumer plan.

Expected decision: verify the policy for the exact plan and settings; do not assume enterprise promises apply.

## Scenario 10 - Accidental exposure

During training, a participant begins uploading a client intake form.

Expected decision: stop immediately, contain the exposure, notify the host contact, document the incident, and follow policy.

# Part C - Confidence Questions

Rate from 1 to 5:

1. I can explain what generative AI does.
2. I can identify information that should not enter an AI tool.
3. I can write a task-specific prompt.
4. I can verify an AI-generated factual output.
5. I can help my organization review an AI tool.
6. I can help draft an interim AI policy.
7. I can document one low-risk AI-assisted workflow.

Compare confidence with performance. A rise in both is ideal. A rise in confidence without a rise in judgment is a curriculum warning light.
