# Sources and Further Reading

These first-party references support the workshop's risk, privacy, verification, and product-behavior guidance. Check current rules and product behavior before delivery.

- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** — A voluntary framework for governing, mapping, measuring, and managing AI risk. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **NIST Generative AI Profile** — Risk considerations and suggested actions specific to generative AI. https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf
- **FTC: Keep your AI claims in check** — Why AI performance and benefit claims need evidence. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check
- **CISA: Stay safe online when using AI** — A short public guide to protecting personal information and checking AI output. https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-09/Secure-Our-World-Using-AI-Tip-Sheet.pdf
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot enterprise data protection** — Current Microsoft documentation on commercial terms, data use, permissions, and plan-dependent controls. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/enterprise-data-protection
