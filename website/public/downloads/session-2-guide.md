# Session 2: The Data Traffic Light

## Purpose

Give teams a quick way to pause, classify information, and ask for authority.

## Learning objectives

- Classify information as green, yellow, or red.
- Separate public availability from organizational permission.
- Notice when harmless facts become identifying after they are combined.
- Pause and escalate uncertain cases.

## Facilitator path

1. **Show the combination problem.** Join names, dates, locations, and circumstances to show how context changes risk.
2. **Classify examples.** Sort public, internal, sensitive, fictional, and uncertain information.
3. **Use the uncertainty rule.** Treat unclear information as yellow or red until someone with authority reviews it.
4. **Map organizational data.** List categories only. Do not copy the underlying data into the worksheet.

## Worksheet prompts

- List three examples of green information.
- List three yellow information categories and the role that may approve them.
- List three red categories without writing any sensitive information.

## Leave with

A draft organizational data rule.
