# Session 5: The Policy Sprint

## Purpose

Turn the prior decisions into a policy that leadership can review and revise.

## Learning objectives

- Define approved, restricted, and prohibited uses.
- Assign ownership for tool approval.
- Set human review and disclosure rules.
- Name an incident reporting path and review date.

## Facilitator path

1. **Read the policy anatomy.** Cover purpose, tools, data, decisions, review, disclosure, incidents, and ownership.
2. **Draft one section at a time.** Use the decisions already made in Sessions 1 through 4.
3. **Test three cases.** Check personal accounts, internal notes, and high-impact recommendations.
4. **Ask the Monday question.** Would a new staff member know what to do at the start of the week?

## Worksheet prompts

- Which uses are approved, restricted, or prohibited?
- Which role approves tools and output?
- How are incidents reported, and when is the policy reviewed?

## Leave with

A one-page interim AI policy.
