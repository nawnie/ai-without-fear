# Session 6: One Safe Workflow

## Purpose

Finish with a small workflow that a team can explain, review, and stop.

## Learning objectives

- Choose a low-impact, reversible, reviewable workflow.
- Define inputs, outputs, tools, review steps, records, and fallback.
- Test with public, fictional, or sanitized information.
- Document what worked, what failed, and what happens next.

## Facilitator path

1. **Pick the workflow.** Choose work based on green data that is small enough to test in one session.
2. **Generate options.** Create several drafts from the same approved inputs.
3. **Select and refine.** Score the options, choose one, and correct one problem at a time.
4. **Finish the record.** Name the owner, checks, approval, storage, retention, fallback, and next review.

## Worksheet prompts

- What is the purpose, owner, approved tool, and allowed input?
- Which checks and approval step are required?
- Where is the output kept, what is retained, and what is the fallback?

## Leave with

A documented workflow and 30-day plan.
