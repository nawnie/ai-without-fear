# Session 4: Tool and Vendor Check

## Purpose

Help organizations choose tools from evidence instead of familiarity or sales copy.

## Learning objectives

- Inventory AI tools used by staff or volunteers.
- Compare tools with the same questions.
- Check account ownership, retention, training use, deletion, export, and admin controls.
- Separate what a tool can do from what the organization permits.

## Facilitator path

1. **Inventory the tools.** Include AI built into office suites, browsers, phones, design tools, and customer systems.
2. **Check one tool.** Use current vendor documentation and record the date checked.
3. **Name the risks.** Review actions, connectors, records, account recovery, and the human fallback.
4. **Make a dated decision.** Approve, limit, pilot, prohibit, or request more information.

## Worksheet prompts

- Who owns the account, and can the organization recover it?
- What happens to prompts, uploads, history, and deleted content?
- Who approves the decision, and when will it be checked again?

## Leave with

A dated tool review and owner.
