# 009 — OneTrainer, FluxGym, kohya_ss, EveryDream2

## Why this card exists

These are the bridge between old image training and modern training.

## OneTrainer

OneTrainer is a one-stop diffusion training GUI. It supports many image models, LoRA, full fine-tuning, embeddings, masked training, and modern model families such as Flux, Flux2, Z-Image, Qwen Image, SDXL, and Hunyuan Video.

AIWF role: GUI alternative worth testing for Windows-first workflows.

## FluxGym

FluxGym is valuable because it shows what a beginner LoRA UI should feel like: enter LoRA info, upload/caption images, click start, optionally generate fixed sample prompts during training.

AIWF role: UX inspiration and narrow Flux trainer.

## kohya_ss / sd-scripts

Still a major foundation for classic SD and SDXL LoRA training and many script conventions. It should be documented as foundational, not treated as the only modern answer.

AIWF role: historical foundation + still-useful classic trainer.

## EveryDream2

Important to Shawn's own history and to the evolution of community training, but mostly a legacy/foundational reference for Vol. 2 unless refreshed by current tests.

AIWF role: history and principles, not primary 2026 recommendation.
