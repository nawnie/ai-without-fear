# Vol. 1 Tone and Voice Model

## Card ID

173

## Why this belongs in Vol. 2 RAG

The house voice is practical, direct, candid, and systems-minded. It is not influencer-thin and not academic fog. It respects beginners by teaching real concepts in a controlled order.

## Continuity rule

Use operator-first explanations: start with the failure, then the principle, then the method, then the lab.

## AIWF editor guidance

- Preserve the field-manual voice.
- Convert abstract ideas into decisions, diagnostics, and labs.
- Mark uncertainty honestly.
- Keep visuals functional.
- Tie every advanced idea back to reader action.

## Vol. 2 application

This card should inform chapter framing, editor prompts, exercise design, and RAG responses when the author asks how to keep Vol. 2 aligned with Vol. 1.
