# 168 — Semi-Supervised and Self-Supervised Learning

**Snapshot:** March 2026  
**Layer:** Statistical learning / generalization / college-postgrad ML  
**Use in AIWF Vol. 2:** Chunk as theory + technique + lab doctrine.

## Core idea

Supervised learning uses labels. Semi-supervised learning mixes labeled and unlabeled data. Self-supervised learning creates training signals from the data itself.

## Modern AI relevance

Foundation models are mostly powered by self-supervised or weakly supervised objectives at scale, then adapted with supervised and preference data.

## AIWF mapping

Embeddings, contrastive learning, masked prediction, denoising diffusion, and next-token prediction are all ways to turn raw data into supervision.

## Exercise

Compare supervised classifier training with embeddings generated by a pretrained self-supervised model plus a small linear classifier.
