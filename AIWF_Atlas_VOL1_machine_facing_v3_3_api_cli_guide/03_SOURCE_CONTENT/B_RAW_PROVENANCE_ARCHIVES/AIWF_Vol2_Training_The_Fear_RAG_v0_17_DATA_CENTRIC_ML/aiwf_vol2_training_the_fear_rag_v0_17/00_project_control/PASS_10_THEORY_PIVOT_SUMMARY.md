## Editorial target

Vol. 1 is middle-school-to-high-school clarity: names, knobs, workflows, safety, and intuition.

Vol. 2 must stay readable, but the endpoint is different: a diligent reader should finish with the functional equivalent of a strong undergraduate ML sequence plus selected graduate-level competence in modern deep learning, generative models, evaluation, and responsible deployment.

## New rule for research passes

Every future pass should include at least one of these:

1. A theory card explaining *why the method works*.
2. A technique card explaining *how to use it correctly*.
3. A failure card explaining *how it breaks*.
4. An exercise card that turns the idea into practice.
5. A source card that ties claims to a primary source, textbook, official documentation, or canonical paper.
